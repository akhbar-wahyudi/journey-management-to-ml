# Journey: Management to ML

A learning log of a management student teaching himself machine learning,
from semester 1 onward.

## About me
I'm Wahyudi, a management student at STIE YKPN Business School. I'm interested in how businesses can combine LLMs (like ChatGPT or Claude) with traditional machine learning, so that automation stays accurate, efficient, and affordable. I'm learning Python, SQL, and ML alongside my degree to understand how these systems actually work.

## Why this repo exists
I want to show my learning process while building systems that combine generative AI with traditional machine learning, and that actually work in real business settings, not just in tutorials.

## What you'll find here
- `semester-1/`: reflections, coursework notes, and ML practice
- More folders will be added each semester as the journey grows
